What is an eSIM and how do I activate it for Italy?

An eSIM is a digital SIM profile already embedded in modern smartphones. Just scan the QR code provided by Airalo or Yesim to connect to high-speed Italian mobile 4G/5G networks immediately upon landing, completely avoiding roaming fees.

Are luggage storage locations in Italy safe and insured?

Yes. Storage points partnered with Radical Storage are verified local businesses (hotels, cafes, shops) that include security seals and insurance up to €3,000 per bag.
