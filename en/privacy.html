Privacy & Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Nature of the Website

SmartTravelHub is an independent, non-commercial personal travel project created for informational and travel-planning purposes across Italy.

The website is maintained privately as a non-incorporated hobby project without a registered business entity. For any inquiries, users may open an issue on the project's public GitHub repository.

2. Personal Data Collected Directly

SmartTravelHub DOES NOT collect, store, or process any direct personal identification data:

  • No contact forms or inquiry fields;
  • No newsletters or email marketing lists;
  • No user account registration or profiles;
  • No names, phone numbers, or credit card details are ever requested or stored.

3. Cookies and Anonymous Analytics

The website utilizes only essential technical local storage and optional aggregated analytics:

  • Consent Memory (localStorage): We store a small key named sth_cookie_consent in your browser solely to remember if you accepted or declined analytics cookies, preventing the banner from reappearing on every click.
  • Google Analytics 4 (Anonymous Traffic): We use Google Analytics to understand aggregate page visits and popular guides. This script is only loaded if you click "Accept" on the cookie banner.
  • GitHub Pages Infrastructure: The site is hosted on GitHub Inc. servers, which may log standard technical web server logs (such as IP addresses) strictly for network security and platform integrity.

4. Affiliate Transparency Disclosure

SmartTravelHub participates in verified travel affiliate partner programs (including Travelpayouts, Klook, Airalo, Radical Storage, and Aviasales).

Clicking on outbound partner links will redirect you to the official provider's secure platform. If you complete a booking, we may earn a small referral commission at zero additional cost to you.

All financial transactions, customer service, and bookings occur solely on the encrypted servers of the third-party providers subject to their independent privacy terms.
