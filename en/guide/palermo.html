🏨 Where to Stay in Palermo

Palermo offers rich Mediterranean contrasts:

  • Politeama & Libertà: Upscale, safe, tree-lined boulevards with luxury shopping and direct airport bus shuttles.
  • Kalsa & Historic Center: Atmospheric alleys, restored noble palaces, artisan workshops, and vibrant nightlife.
  • Mondello (Summer Base): Famous white-sand beach resort just 20 minutes from downtown.

🚆 Getting to Palermo: Falcone Borsellino Airport (PMO)

From Falcone-Borsellino Airport (PMO) in Punta Raisi, the dedicated Genio Express train connects to Palermo Centrale in 35-45 minutes. Alternatively, direct airport buses run to Piazza Politeama every 30 minutes for €6.

🏛️ Norman Palace, Palatine Chapel & Monreale

The shimmering Byzantine gold mosaics inside the Palatine Chapel (Cappella Palatina), the grandeur of Teatro Massimo (Italy's largest opera house), and the nearby UNESCO cathedral of Monreale are world-class highlights. Booking skip-the-line tickets in advance avoids long queues.

💡 4 Practical Tips Foreigners Must Know in Palermo

  • Historic Street Markets: Visit Ballarò, Capo, and Vucciria markets. Street vendors sing and chant in Sicilian dialect (abbanniate) selling freshly fried panelle, arancine, and sfincione pizza.
  • Capuchin Catacombs: An extraordinary underground site preserving over 8,000 naturally mummified bodies from the 16th to 19th centuries, including the famous Rosalia Lombardo.
  • Authentic Cannoli: Real Sicilian cannoli are always filled with sweet fresh ricotta cheese right in front of you—never pre-filled, so the pastry shell stays crispy.
  • Day Trip to Cefalù: Direct coastal trains reach the idyllic seaside beach village of Cefalù in just 50 minutes from Palermo Centrale for €7.
