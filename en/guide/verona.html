Must-See Attractions in Verona

  • Verona Arena (Arena di Verona): A remarkably preserved 1st-century Roman amphitheater famous worldwide for hosting large-scale opera performances.
  • Juliet's House (Casa di Giulietta): Visit the legendary 13th-century Gothic building featuring the iconic balcony associated with Shakespeare's Romeo and Juliet.
  • Piazza delle Erbe & Torre dei Lamberti: The historic heart of Verona, surrounded by frescoed buildings and featuring a tower with breathtaking city views.
  • Castelvecchio Bridge (Ponte Scaligero): Walk across the fortified red-brick bridge spanning the Adige River for iconic photo spots.

Practical Tips for Tourists

Transportation: Verona Porta Nuova is the main railway station with high-speed connections to Venice (1h) and Milan (1h 15m). The city center is easily walkable from the station in 15-20 minutes.

Flight Disruptions: If your flight to/from Verona Airport (VRN) is delayed over 3 hours or canceled, check your legal eligibility for compensation up to €600 via AirHelp.
