🏨 Where to Stay in Verona (Best Areas)

Staying in the Historic Center (Piazza Bra and Piazza delle Erbe) allows you to walk back to your hotel right after the evening opera shows at the Arena. The San Zeno district offers a charming residential vibe with traditional Venetian osterias, while the area around Porta Nuova Station is ideal for modern hotels at great rates and fast connections to Lake Garda and Venice.

📜 Veronese Secrets and Curiosities

  • The Costa Arch and the Mysterious Bone: Between Piazza delle Erbe and Piazza dei Signori, a whale bone (or prehistoric reptile bone) has been hanging for centuries. Legend says it will only drop when someone completely free of sin walks underneath!
  • The Well of Love: A hidden alley near Piazza delle Erbe holds this well linked to the tragic 16th-century love story between Corrado and Isabella.
  • The View from Castel San Pietro: Taking the funicular or walking up the stairs past Ponte Pietra leads to the ultimate viewpoint to admire the sunset over the Adige River.
