🏛️ Egyptian Museum & Mole Antonelliana Cinema Museum

Turin’s Egyptian Museum (Museo Egizio) is the world's oldest and second-largest collection of Egyptian artifacts outside Cairo. The landmark Mole Antonelliana houses the National Cinema Museum with a panoramic glass elevator rising to a breathtaking viewing deck overlooking the Alps.

💡 4 Practical Tips Foreigners Must Know in Turin

  • Try the Bicerin in Historic Cafes: Since 1763, Turin serves Bicerin—a layered hot drink of espresso, melted dark chocolate, and whole milk cream. Visit historic spots like Caffè Al Bicerin or Baratti & Milano.
  • White & Black Magic Lore: Turin is renowned as an esoteric crossroads: a vertex of the white magic triangle (with Lyon and Prague) and black magic (with London and San Francisco). Look for the Devil's Door in Palazzo Trucchi di Levaldigi.
  • Gianduiotto Chocolate: Turin invented hazelnut milk chocolate. Sample authentic Gianduiotti at historic confectioners throughout the center.
  • Langhe Wine Day Trip: Rent a car to explore the nearby UNESCO-listed rolling hills of Barolo, Barbaresco, and Alba in under an hour.
