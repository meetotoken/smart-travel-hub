Florence: The Renaissance Capital Handbook for Smart Travelers

Where to stay, Uffizi skip-the-line passes, luggage storage, and secret wine windows.

🏨 Where to Stay in Florence (Top Neighborhoods)

Florence is a remarkably walkable city where you rarely need public transit if you pick the right base:

  • Duomo & San Marco: The historic core. You are steps away from the cathedral and Uffizi, ideal for short 1-2 day stays.
  • Oltrarno (Santo Spirito & San Frediano): Across the Arno river. Famous for traditional artisan workshops, vintage boutiques, authentic trattorias, and lively evening piazzas.
  • Santa Maria Novella (SMN Station Area): Outstanding choice if you plan day trips to Siena, Pisa, or the Chianti wine region, offering great value boutique hotels.

🚆 Getting to Florence by High-Speed Train & Airports

Firenze Santa Maria Novella (SMN) station is right in the city center. Frequent high-speed trains connect Florence to Rome in just 1 hour 30 minutes, and to Venice in 2 hours 15 minutes. If flying into Florence Peretola Airport (FLR), the T2 tramway connects you to the central station in 20 minutes for under €2. If landing in Pisa (PSA), direct airport buses and trains run every 30 minutes.

🧳 Secure Luggage Storage near SMN Station

Florence's historical streets are paved with uneven flagstones, and dragging suitcases up hotel stairs or through crowded museum queues is forbidden. Drop your luggage at verified storage spots right outside SMN station or near Piazza della Signoria from just €5/day.

🏛️ Uffizi Gallery, Accademia & Brunelleschi's Dome

The Uffizi Gallery (home to Botticelli's Birth of Venus) and the Accademia Gallery (housing Michelangelo’s original 17-foot David) strictly regulate visitor capacity. Waiting in the non-reserved queue can waste over 2 hours of your vacation. Always pre-book timed-entry skip-the-line vouchers.

💡 4 Practical Tips Foreigners Must Know in Florence

  • Historic Wine Windows (Buchette del Vino): Look closely at palace walls for tiny arched windows. Used in the 1600s plague to sell Chianti wine without human contact, several have reopened today to serve glasses of wine, Aperol Spritz, and gelato directly to the street!
  • Beware of ZTL Fines if Driving: The entire historical center of Florence is an active ZTL (Restricted Traffic Zone). Rental cars without permits will be automatically photographed and fined €100+ per entry. Park outside the center.
  • Sunset at Piazzale Michelangelo: Walk up the hill (or take bus 12/13) to Piazzale Michelangelo for the single best panoramic sunset view over Florence and the Arno river.
  • Leather Market Bargaining: At San Lorenzo leather market, polite bargaining is expected. Always smell and check the leather for genuine "Made in Italy" stamps before buying.
