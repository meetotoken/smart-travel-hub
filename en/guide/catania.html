🌋 Mount Etna Volcano Summit Tours & The Fish Market

Mount Etna is Europe's highest active volcano. Taking an authorized 4x4 jeep tour or cable car trek to the smoking summit craters, followed by exploring the chaotic historic Fish Market (A Piscaria) behind Piazza del Duomo, are unmissable experiences.

💡 4 Practical Tips Foreigners Must Know in Catania

  • Liotru the Magic Elephant: In Piazza del Duomo stands Catania’s ancient symbol: an elephant sculpted from black lava basalt. Legend says it was carved by the 8th-century sorcerer Eliodoro.
  • Underground Amenano River: The subterranean Amenano river, buried by the 1669 lava flow, resurfaces at the fountain in Piazza Duomo, pouring like a transparent water sheet before plunging underground again.
  • Granita & Brioche Breakfast: Join the local morning ritual: order almond or pistachio granita served with a warm, fluffy brioche with a top knot (brioche col tuppo).
  • Day Trip to Taormina: Frequent trains connect Catania Centrale to Taormina-Giardini in just 45 minutes for under €6.
