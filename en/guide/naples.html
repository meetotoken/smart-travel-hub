🏨 Where to Stay in Naples (Best Districts)

Naples is raw, historic, and bursting with character:

  • Chiaia & Lungomare Caracciolo: The upscale seaside district. Waterfront promenades, luxury hotels, peaceful nights, and views of Mount Vesuvius and Capri.
  • Centro Storico (Spaccanapoli): Immersed in centuries of history, street food, and buzzing alleys. Ideal for walking to monuments.
  • Piazza Garibaldi (Napoli Centrale): The most practical hub for catching the Circumvesuviana train to Pompeii and Sorrento, offering budget-friendly boutique B&Bs.

🚆 Arriving at Napoli Centrale & Capodichino Airport (NAP)

High-speed trains from Rome arrive at Napoli Centrale in just 1 hour 10 minutes. If landing at Naples International Airport (NAP), the dedicated Alibus shuttle connects directly to Napoli Centrale and the ferry port (Molo Beverello) in 15-20 minutes for €5.

🏛️ Day Trips to Pompeii, Underground Naples & Veiled Christ

The marble masterpiece of the Veiled Christ (Cristo Velato) inside the Sansevero Chapel requires reservations weeks ahead. For day trips to the ancient ruins of Pompeii & Herculaneum, taking the local Circumvesuviana train or pre-booking a skip-the-line guided bus tour from central Naples saves significant time.

💡 4 Practical Tips Foreigners Must Know in Naples

  • Pizza "a Portafoglio": Naples is the birthplace of pizza. In the historic center, order a freshly baked Margherita folded like a wallet (a portafoglio) for just €2.50–€3.50.
  • Crossing the Street: Motor scooters weave dynamically through traffic. To cross the street safely like a local, make eye contact with approaching drivers and walk steadily without sudden stops.
  • Napoli Sotterranea (Underground): Explore the fascinating network of Roman aqueducts and WWII bomb shelters 40 meters beneath the modern city streets.
  • Caffè Sospeso (Suspended Coffee): A beloved local tradition where generous patrons pay for an extra cup of coffee in advance for someone in need who comes into the cafe later.
