🏨 Where to Stay in Milan (Best Neighborhoods)

Milan balances sleek modern design with historic elegance:

  • Navigli: Picturesque canal district famed for bohemian nightlife, vibrant aperitivo culture, and artisan boutiques.

🏛️ Leonardo's Last Supper & Duomo Rooftops

Leonardo da Vinci’s masterpiece **The Last Supper** (at Santa Maria delle Grazie) sells out months ahead and requires strict timed bookings. For the **Duomo Cathedral**, climbing or taking the elevator up to the white marble rooftops offers magnificent views over the city skyline.

💡 4 Practical Tips Foreigners Must Know in Milan

  • Aperitivo Culture: In areas like Navigli and Brera, ordering an evening cocktail (like a Negroni or Spritz) around 7 PM usually includes access to generous buffet spreads or finger food.
  • Validating Metro Tickets: Milan's contactless turnstiles accept credit cards directly at metro gates, but paper tickets must be stamped inside buses and trams.
  • Day Trip to Lake Como: Regional trains from Milano Centrale reach Lake Como (Varenna or Como town) in just 1 hour for under €8 each way.
  • Galleria Superstition: For good luck, spin your heel three times right over the mosaic bull's testicles embedded in the floor of Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II.
