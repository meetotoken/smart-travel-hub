Duomo rooftop passes, Da Vinci's Last Supper tickets, airport rail, and Lake Como connections.

🏨 Where to Stay in Milan (Best Neighborhoods)

Milan is clean, fast-paced, and easily navigable via its modern subway system:

  • Brera: Picturesque, romantic paved streets, art galleries, high-end boutiques, and sidewalk cafes. Perfect for couples.
  • Navigli: The canal district. Best area for vibrant nightlife, craft cocktail bars, and evening canal-side dining.
  • Centrale & Porta Nuova: Practical travel hubs. Outstanding luxury and budget hotel options with instant rail connections to Malpensa Airport, Lake Como, and high-speed trains to Venice and Florence.

🚆 Getting to Milan: 3 Airports & Rail Hubs

Milan has three airports: Malpensa (MXP) (connected to Centrale and Cadorna stations by the Malpensa Express train in 50 minutes), Linate (LIN) (conveniently connected via the new M4 Blue Metro line in 12 minutes to downtown), and Bergamo Orio al Serio (BGY) (budget flight hub served by frequent 50-minute express buses).

🏛️ Duomo Rooftop & The Last Supper (Mandatory Reservations)

Leonardo da Vinci’s masterpiece, The Last Supper (Il Cenacolo), inside the refectory of Santa Maria delle Grazie has an extreme visitor cap: only 35 people are admitted every 15 minutes. Tickets sell out months in advance. Visiting the Duomo Rooftop Terraces on foot or via elevator is the quintessential Milan experience and requires timed vouchers.

💡 4 Practical Tips Foreigners Must Know in Milan

  • Aperitivo Tradition: Between 6:30 PM and 9:00 PM in the Navigli or Brera district, ordering a drink (Spritz, Negroni, wine for €10–€15) includes access to a lavish buffet of pasta, pizza, cheeses, and finger foods.
  • Contactless Metro Payments: You don't need paper tickets for Milan's subway: simply tap any contactless credit card, Apple Pay, or Google Wallet on the turnstile readers.
  • Spinning on the Bull of Galleria: In the center of Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II, look for the mosaic bull on the floor. Tradition says spinning on your right heel 3 times over its testicles brings good luck!
  • Day Trip to Lake Como in 40 Minutes: Direct regional trains run from Milano Centrale to Varenna-Esino on Lake Como in just 40 minutes for under €8 each way.
