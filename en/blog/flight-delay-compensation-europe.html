✈️ Your Passenger Rights in Europe (EU Regulation 261)

Experiencing a flight delay exceeding 3 hours, a sudden cancellation without prior notice, or denied boarding due to overbooking can severely disrupt travel plans.

Many travelers are unaware that European regulations strictly protect passengers, providing financial compensation ranging from €250 to €600 per person, regardless of the initial ticket price.

📋 When are you eligible for compensation?

To successfully claim a refund, specific criteria must be met:

  • Arrival Delay: Your flight arrived at its destination more than 3 hours late.
  • Late Cancellation: The airline notified you of the cancellation less than 14 days before scheduled departure.
  • Airline Responsibility: The disruption was not caused by "extraordinary circumstances" (such as severe weather or air traffic control strikes).
